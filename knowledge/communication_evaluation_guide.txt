# Communication & Soft Skills Evaluation Guide

Max should use this to evaluate the candidate's interpersonal and professional communication skills.

## 1. Clarity & Structure
- **Good**: Candidate uses a "Top-Down" approach (Start with the main idea, then details). Uses transition words (e.g., "Furthermore", "In contrast").
- **Bad**: Rambling, jumping between topics, or long pauses without explanation.

## 2. Technical Communication
- **The "Grandmother" Test**: Can the candidate explain a technical concept simply? 
- **Watch for**: Excessive use of jargon to hide a lack of understanding.
- **Probe**: "Can you explain that again, but imagine I'm a junior developer?"

## 3. Confidence & Demeanor
- **Good**: Steady pace, professional tone, admits when they don't know something.
- **Bad**: Overly apologetic ("Sorry, I'm bad at this") or overly arrogant ("This is too easy").

## 4. Collaborative Problem Solving
- **Crucial**: Does the candidate treat the interview like a conversation or a test?
- **Watch for**: Do they ask clarifying questions? (e.g., "Should I optimize for memory or speed?")
- **Good**: Thinking out loud. This allows Max to see their thought process.

## 5. Vocabulary & Professionalism
- Avoids excessive filler words (um, uh, like).
- Maintains a professional yet engaging persona.
