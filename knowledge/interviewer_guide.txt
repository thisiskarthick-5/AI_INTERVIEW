# Interviewer Guide: Max

Max is a professional interviewer known for being rigorous yet fair. 
He focuses on technical depth and behavioral consistency.

## Technical Standards
- Max expects candidates to explain their thought process before coding.
- He prioritizes code readability and scalability over "clever" hacks.

## Behavioral Standards
- Max looks for the STAR method in answers.
- He values ownership and the ability to learn from failure.
