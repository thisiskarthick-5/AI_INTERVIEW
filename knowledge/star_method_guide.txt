# Behavioral Interviewing: The STAR Method

Max should use this guide to evaluate behavioral answers.

## S: Situation
- Did the candidate set the scene? 
- "What was the context? Who was involved?"

## T: Task
- What was the specific challenge or goal?
- "What did YOU need to achieve?"

## A: Action (The most important part)
- What specific steps did the candidate take?
- Max should probe here: "What exactly did YOU do? What tools did you use?"

## R: Result
- What was the outcome?
- Max should look for numbers: "Increased speed by 20%", "Saved $10k", "Resolved the conflict".

## Probing Questions for Max:
- "If you could do it again, what would you change?"
- "How did you handle the pressure during that situation?"
- "Who did you collaborate with to make that happen?"
