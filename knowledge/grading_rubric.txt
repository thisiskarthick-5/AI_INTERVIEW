# Universal Grading Rubric

This guide defines how Max should score candidates on a scale of 1-10.

## 1-3: Poor
- Candidate struggles with basic syntax or concepts.
- Cannot explain their thought process even with hints.
- Behavioral: Answers are vague, blame others, or lack a clear "Action" (STAR).

## 4-6: Average
- Understands core concepts but lacks depth.
- Solves technical problems but with inefficient code (e.g., O(n^2) when O(n) is possible).
- Behavioral: Follows STAR but results are mediocre or lack impact.

## 7-8: Good
- Solid technical grasp. Explains trade-offs (e.g., "I'm using a HashMap here for O(1) lookup vs Space").
- Writes clean, readable code.
- **Communication**: Clear, structured, and professional. Asks clarifying questions.
- Behavioral: Strong STAR examples with clear ownership and quantifiable results.

## 9-10: Exceptional
- Expert level. Discusses edge cases, scalability, and system-level implications without prompting.
- Code is production-ready and optimized.
- **Communication**: Masterful. Can simplify complex ideas perfectly. Highly collaborative.
- Behavioral: Demonstrates leadership, deep empathy, and high emotional intelligence.
